VIVA PREPARATION — QUESTION BANK
Signal Generator & Analyzer (SP24)
=====================================
(Viva is 50% of the grade — see course_faq_reference.md. Every team
member must be able to answer these individually, not just the person
who wrote that part of the code. See questions_plus_answer.txt for
model answers tied to this codebase.)

SECTION 1 — DSP FUNDAMENTALS
-----------------------------
1.1  What is a signal? What is the difference between an analog and a
     digital signal?
1.2  What is sampling? State the Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem.
1.3  What is the Nyquist rate / Nyquist frequency for a given signal?
1.4  What is aliasing? How would you demonstrate it using this app?
1.5  Why do the plots in this app "look" continuous when the underlying
     signal is a discrete array of samples?
1.6  What is the difference between amplitude, frequency, phase, and
     period of a periodic signal?
1.7  What is duty cycle, and which waveform in this app uses it?
1.8  What is a chirp signal? Where are chirp signals used in practice?
1.9  Why is the sinc function important in signal processing (its
     relationship to the ideal low-pass filter / brick-wall filter)?
1.10 What happens mathematically to a sinc pulse as its frequency
     parameter increases?
1.11 What is RMS (root mean square) of a signal, and what does it
     represent physically (e.g., in terms of power)?
1.12 What is peak-to-peak amplitude, and how does it differ from RMS?
1.13 What is quantization, and where does it occur in this project?

SECTION 2 — WAVEFORM GENERATION (src/generator/waveforms.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------

     Why divide by sample_rate instead of multiplying?
2.2  How is the sine wave generated? Write/derive its formula.

     of sin()? Explain the modulo-based phase computation.

     while the frequency term is linear.

     `duration_s / 2`?

     for any of these generators?

     do you derive it from the phase function (dφ/dt)?

SECTION 3 — FREQUENCY-DOMAIN ANALYSIS (src/analyzer/freq_domain.py)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  What is the Fourier Transform, and why is it used to analyze a
     signal generated in the time domain?
3.2  What is the FFT (Fast Fourier Transform), and how does it differ
     algorithmically from the DFT?
3.3  Why does the code use `np.fft.rfft` instead of `np.fft.fft`?
3.4  What is a "single-sided" spectrum, and why is it valid to discard
     the negative-frequency half for a real-valued signal?
3.5  How are the frequency bin values computed (`np.fft.rfftfreq`)?
     What determines the frequency resolution of the FFT?
3.6  Why is the FFT magnitude normalized by dividing by `n` (the number
     of samples)?
3.7  Explain the `to_db()` function: why 20*log10, why normalize to the
     spectrum's own peak, and why floor at -100 dB?
3.8  What would the FFT of a pure sine wave look like versus the FFT of
     a square wave? Why does a square wave have odd harmonics?
3.9  What is spectral leakage, and could it occur in this analyzer
     (no windowing is applied before the FFT)?
3.10 If you doubled the duration (and hence number of samples) of a
     signal at a fixed sample rate, how would the FFT's frequency
     resolution change?
